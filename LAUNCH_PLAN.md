# PowerNap Toggler Launch Plan

## Positioning

PowerNap Toggler is a tiny macOS menu bar utility for switching between Battery Saver and Normal Mode by controlling Power Nap and TCP keepalive.

## Launch Offer

- Launch as free for the first 30 days.
- After the launch window, move to a one-time paid license.
- Recommended paid price: $9 once.
- No subscription for the first paid release.

## Core Claims

Allowed:
- Tiny macOS menu bar utility
- Switch between Battery Saver and Normal Mode
- Controls Power Nap and TCP keepalive
- Runs on-device
- No tracking
- Direct download, no App Store required

Avoid:
- Fake download counts
- Fake reviews
- Unsupported battery-life promises
- Claims that the app is notarized before it is
- Pro features that are not built

## Launch Copy

Short:

> PowerNap Toggler is free for launch month. Tiny macOS menu bar app. Switch your Mac between Battery Saver and Normal Mode. No tracking. No App Store required.

Medium:

> PowerNap Toggler is a lean macOS menu bar utility that switches Power Nap and TCP keepalive between Battery Saver and Normal Mode. It is free during launch month, then moves to a simple one-time license.
