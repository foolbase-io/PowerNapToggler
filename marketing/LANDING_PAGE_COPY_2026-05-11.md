## FAQ

### Does this guarantee better battery life?

No. Mac sleep drain can have several causes. PowerNap Toggler focuses on Power Nap and TCP keepalive, which can be part of the background wake/network behavior story.

### Why does macOS ask for an admin password?

PowerNap Toggler changes system energy and networking settings, so macOS requires administrator permission before applying changes.

### Is it tracking me?

No. PowerNap Toggler runs on-device and does not include in-app tracking.

### Is it on the App Store?

No. It is distributed directly as a ZIP that is signed and notarized for Gatekeeper.
