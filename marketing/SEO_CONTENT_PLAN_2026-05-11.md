## Page Rules

- Explain first, sell second.
- Include manual command alternatives.
- Never promise exact battery improvement.
- Mention that sleep drain has multiple causes.
- Use “can be part of the story,” not “is the cause.”
- Keep CTA soft and relevant.
- Include the current Apple compatibility nuance: Apple's support guide says the Power Nap option is only available on Intel-based Mac computers.
