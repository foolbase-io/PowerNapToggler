# Product Hunt Draft

## Name

PowerNap Toggler

## Tagline

Tiny Mac menu bar switch for Battery Saver and Normal Mode

## Description

PowerNap Toggler is a lightweight macOS menu bar utility that lets you switch Power Nap and TCP keepalive between Battery Saver and Normal Mode from one compact control panel.

It is built for Mac users who want their laptop to rest when they care about battery, but return to normal background behavior when they need the Mac ready and connected.

Free through June 8, 2026. No tracking. No App Store required.

## First Comment

Hey Product Hunt,

I built PowerNap Toggler as a tiny macOS utility for a very specific annoyance: when your Mac keeps doing background wake/network activity and you just want a simple way to control it.

The app switches two built-in macOS settings:

- Power Nap
- TCP keepalive

There are only two modes:

- **Battery Saver**: quiets those background behaviors.
- **Normal Mode**: turns them back on.

I rebuilt the app with a cleaner menu bar panel, verified state handling, clearer permission messaging, and no tracking.

It is free through June 8, 2026 while I collect feedback before switching to a $9 one-time license.

Would love feedback from MacBook users, indie devs, and anyone who has fought weird battery drain during sleep.
